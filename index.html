ClipPilot

A personal app that turns the best moments of livestreams into vertical short videos and posts them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

What it does

  • Finds the moments viewers reacted to most in a stream recording, using the chat, the audio and an AI judge.
  • Edits them into 9:16 clips with captions.
  • Lets its owner review every clip. Only approved clips are posted, at the times the owner picks.
  • Posts them to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account through the platforms' official APIs, and shows each post's views and likes.

Who uses it

ClipPilot runs only on its owner's own computer. There is no ClipPilot server, no sign-up and nothing to buy. It is only used for streams the owner made or has written permission to clip, and every caption credits the streamer.

YouTube

ClipPilot uses YouTube API Services. When its owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipPilot asks Google for permission to upload videos, and to see the channel's videos so it can show their views and likes. By using ClipPilot's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The privacy policy explains what happens with this data.
