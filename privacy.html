Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

ClipPilot is a personal app that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what data it uses, why, where it's kept and how to remove it.

Data from Google (YouTube)

ClipPilot uses YouTube API Services. When you connect your YouTube channel, Google asks you to allow:

  • Upload videos (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload): to upload the short videos you approve, with their title, description and tags.
  • View your YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly): to read the upload status and the view, like and comment counts of the videos ClipPilot posted, and show them to you in the app.

ClipPilot doesn't read your other videos, your messages or anyone else's personal data.

Where it's stored

The sign-in Google gives ClipPilot (an OAuth token) and the stats it reads are saved only on your computer, in ClipPilot's data folder. They are never sent to us or to anyone else: ClipPilot has no server.

How it's used and shared

  • Google user data is used only to post the clips you approve and to show their stats to you.
  • It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.
  • ClipPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The videos you clip

To find highlights, ClipPilot sends still frames, the transcript and the chat of the moments you choose to clip to the AI service its owner set up (NVIDIA's API). Your Google sign-in and your channel's stats are never sent there.

Instagram and TikTok

Connecting Instagram (Meta) or TikTok works the same way: the sign-in is stored only on your computer and is used only to post the clips you approve and, where available, to read their view and like counts.

Removing access and data

  • You can remove ClipPilot's access to your Google account at any time on Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Deleting ClipPilot's data folder deletes everything it saved, including the sign-ins.

Cookies

ClipPilot and this website don't use cookies, analytics or trackers. The website's host may keep standard server logs.
